What Fresh Start Report is

A morning market snapshot for people who buy and sell fresh fruit and vegetables: growers, shippers, distributors, and chain-store and terminal-market buyers.

  • USDA FOB shipping-point prices on a 7-day moving average, across every shipping point in production, including Mexico crossings through Nogales, Otay Mesa, and Texas
  • Live weather in the production areas, right next to the pricing
  • Three years of same-date history, so you know whether today's number is high, low, or where the season says it should be
  • An evening digest of the USDA reports for the views you follow

Market data is sourced from the USDA. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com. Built by produce professionals, for produce professionals.
